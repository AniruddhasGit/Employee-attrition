## What this session did

- **Accounts:**
  - Two roles behind one sign-in page.
  - Sessions are cookie-based, with argon2id password hashing and a CSRF check on the request Origin.
  - The browser talks only to the web origin; the Next.js route `/api/[...path]` proxies to the API.
  - The login limit counts failed attempts only, per IP and per (IP, email).
- **HR area:**
  - `/hr` dashboard: KPIs, cost at stake, "Sharp drops since last check-in", people table, heatmap, "What would keep people here", trust panel.
  - `/hr/people/[id]` person page: drivers, risk history, check-in answers, what would keep them, action plans with estimate and actual change, what-if.
  - `/hr/check-ins`: response rates, people who haven't responded, "You said, we did" composer.
- **Employee area:** `/me` home and a 9-step quarterly check-in at `/me/check-in`. Employees never get a risk score from any `/api/me` endpoint.
- **Seeded history:**
  - Covers three closed quarters plus the current open one, always relative to today.
  - Five accounts (1 HR, 4 employees), each telling a story.
  - A daily reset, and history regenerates at startup when a new quarter begins.
  - The 237 leavers are treated as former staff.
- **Model:**
  - Tuned XGBoost hyperparameters, searched inside the training split only.
  - Canonical Linux held-out results: ROC-AUC 0.814, PR-AUC 0.600, Brier 0.096, threshold 0.227.
  - The model card has a tuning section with paired-bootstrap evidence. The gain is modest.
- **Landing page:**
  - Living hero crowd: sway, check-in waves, leavers caught and brought back, cursor lantern.
  - Before/after section that auto-plays and can be dragged.
  - "How it works" loop section.
- **Polish and copy:** a design polish pass on every screen; no "Try" or "demo" wording in the UI.
- **Credentials:** demo credentials are private. There is no public account list, and the password syncs from `DEMO_PASSWORD` at startup.
